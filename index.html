Privacy

Your data should belong to you. Private by default where that’s possible, honest where it isn’t.

  • Local processing

    Compatible models run on your computer. In Local mode, prompts and files stay on your device.

  • Optional cloud

    Cloud models are an add-on, never a requirement. Connect a provider only if you want more power.

  • Transparent handling

    Every answer shows where it ran: on your computer or with a named cloud provider.

  • You control the cloud

    pleeply is designed to ask before the first request goes to any provider, and cloud can be switched off entirely.

The honest partWhen you choose a cloud model, your request is processed on that provider’s servers under their privacy policy. pleeply can’t make cloud processing private. It can make sure you always know when it happens.

Questions

Can I download pleeply today?

Not yet. pleeply Desktop is in development. Join early access and we’ll email you when invites start. We won’t promise a date we can’t keep.

Will any AI model run on my computer?

No. What runs locally depends on your memory and graphics. Small models (roughly 1–8B parameters) run on many recent laptops with 16 GB of memory; larger ones need more memory or a strong GPU. pleeply is designed to check your hardware and recommend models that fit.

Does my data leave my computer?

In Local mode, no: prompts and files are processed on your device. When you choose a cloud model, your request goes to that provider and their privacy policy applies. pleeply is designed to ask before that happens.

What are Saturn, Nova, Atlas and Orbit?

Planned names for model setups inside pleeply, each aimed at a different kind of work. None of them is released yet, and they are not foundation models we trained from scratch.

How much will it cost?

We haven’t set pricing for pleeply Desktop. Joining early access is free and doesn’t commit you to anything.

What is pleeply Pocket?

A small device that would run pleeply at home. It is in design, with planned prices from about $299 to $549. There’s no waitlist for it yet: pleeply Desktop comes first.
