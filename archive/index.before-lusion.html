Principles

Built on five rules.

01

Private by design

Privacy is in the architecture, not in a settings menu. Your voice is processed by the chip inside the speaker.

02

Your own AI

Your device, your data, your rules. No big-tech account required.

03

Local intelligence

Models run in your home, so answers don't wait on a server on another continent.

04

No cloud required

Core features work without internet. Connect online services only if you choose to.

05

Open models

Run compatible open models — Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma — and swap them as they get better.
