The difference

Where does your voice go?

A typical smart speaker

  1. 1You speak
  2. 2Audio is sent to company servers
  3. 3Processed in their cloud
  4. 4The answer comes back

No internet, no assistant.

pleeply

  1. 1You speak
  2. 2The computer inside the speaker thinks
  3. 3You get the answer

Nothing leaves the room unless you allow it.

Principles

Privacy is built in, not bolted on.

Show me the device
  1. 01

    Private by design

    Privacy is in the architecture, not in a settings menu. Your voice is processed by the chip inside the speaker.

  2. 02

    Your own AI

    Your device, your data, your rules. No big-tech account required.

  3. 03

    Local intelligence

    Models run in your home, so answers don't wait on a server on another continent.

  4. 04

    No cloud required

    Core features work without internet. Connect online services only if you choose to.

  5. 05

    Open models

    Run compatible open models — Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma — and swap them as they get better.
