What is pleeply?

Updated Oct 25, 20263 min read View .md

pleeply is a speaker with a computer inside. It runs open-weight AI models on the device, keeps a private memory of you at home and works without the cloud. pleeply is in development and not for sale yet; the waitlist is open at pleeply.vercel.app.

Status: in development

Prices below are expected prices and specs are planned — both may change during development. Reserving is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

How it works#

  1. You talk. Ask anything, set timers, control your home, think out loud.
  2. It thinks on the device. An open language model runs on the computer inside the speaker — no round trip to a cloud server.
  3. It remembers, privately. Your personal memory lives on the device. See it, edit it, delete it anytime.

Principles#

  • Private by design. Your voice is processed by the chip inside the speaker.
  • Your own AI. Your device, your data, your rules — no big-tech account required.
  • Local intelligence. Models run in your home, so answers don't wait on a server on another continent.
  • No cloud required. Core features work without internet; connect online services only if you choose to.
  • Open models. Run compatible open models — Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma — and swap them as they get better.

FAQ#

Is pleeply available to buy?#

Not yet. pleeply is in development. You can reserve one for free on the waitlist, and we'll email you when reservations open.

How much will pleeply cost?#

Expected prices are $499 for pleeply Pocket (a hand-assembled, pocket-size device with a Raspberry Pi 5), $299 for the pleeply One speaker and $549 for pleeply Pro. Prices and specs may change during development.

Does pleeply work offline?#

That's the design goal: core features — talking to it, answers from the on-device model and your memory — work without internet. Online services are optional.

Which AI models does pleeply run?#

Compatible open-weight models such as Llama, Qwen, Mistral and Gemma. The One is planned for compact models up to about 8B parameters, the Pro for larger models up to about 32B.

Where is my data stored?#

On the device. Your voice is processed by the computer inside the speaker, and your personal memory lives there too. You can see, edit or delete it anytime.

How is pleeply different from Alexa?#

Alexa processes requests in Amazon's cloud — since March 28, 2025 there has been no setting to keep them on the device. pleeply runs its model on the speaker and keeps your memory at home.
