Hi Raspberry Pi team,

I'm building pleeply, a speaker with its own AI: open models run on the device, it keeps a private memory of its owner, and it works without the cloud. We're building our prototypes on Raspberry Pi 5 with the AI HAT+ 2. The Hailo-10H is exactly what makes a local, private voice assistant practical at this size.

We opened the waitlist this week (pleeply.vercel.app) and are running a 14-day demand test before building hardware.

I'd like to propose a collaboration with Raspberry Pi as the main sponsor of our prototype stage:

What we'd ask for
- 10 × Raspberry Pi 5 and 10 × AI HAT+ 2 for prototypes and our first beta testers.

What we'd give back
- Our prototypes are built on Raspberry Pi and we say so everywhere: the product page, build logs and launch materials.
- An open-source pleeply OS image for Pi 5 + AI HAT+ 2, so anyone can build a private voice assistant with local memory.
- A joint launch post and a build write-up for the Raspberry Pi blog or The MagPi, with real benchmarks: tokens per second and voice response latency.

Happy to share the waitlist numbers at the end of the test, or jump on a short call.

Thanks,
Arnur
